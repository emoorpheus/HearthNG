# Using HearthNG

## The remote keys

| Where | Key | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Home row | Left / Right | Move along the row. At the end it goes round to the start |
| Home row | OK | Open the app |
| Home row | hold OK | Edit mode: Left / Right move the app, Up gives *Hide*, *Uninstall* and the favourites star; OK or Back: done |
| Home | Back | The **Recent apps** panel (OK opens an app, hold OK closes it, Up is *Close all*) |
| Settings | Left / Right on the tab bar | Change tab |
| Settings | OK, or Left / Right | OK steps through choices; the arrows change numbers |
| Any other screen | Back | One step back |

The last tile of the row is **HearthNG Settings**. Two more tiles, *LG Home* (opens LG's own home screen) and *System Settings* (the TV's own settings), are hidden at first. Switch them on in **Settings, Look, Home row**.

![The app menu of the original Hearth (HearthNG's edit mode draws arrows and round buttons at the tile instead)](../screenshots/app-menu.jpg)

## Choose which apps show, and in what order

Open **Settings, Apps & Sources**. You see every app and every input (HDMI 1, HDMI 2 ...). Press **OK** on one to show or hide it. The buttons above the grid:

- **Refresh list:** looks for apps you installed a minute ago.
- **Favourites:** see below.
- **Uninstall apps:** (Apps tab, rooted TV only.) Press it, then press **OK** on an app and confirm. The app is removed from the TV for good. Apps that belong to the TV, and HearthNG itself, cannot be removed; HearthNG tells you why. Press **Done** (or Back) when you finish.
- **Order by last opened:** a switch. The apps you used last jump to the front. While it is on, the arrows of edit mode do not move apps. Switch it off and your own order is back.

## Put the apps in your own order

On the Home screen hold **OK** on an app: edit mode starts and arrows appear either side of the tile. Press **Left** or **Right** to move it, **OK** or **Back** when you are done. **Up** goes to the round buttons above the tile: *Hide*, *Uninstall* (apps that can be removed) and a star for the favourites. While the HearthNG Settings tile is hidden (*Settings, Look, Home row, Hide the HearthNG Settings tile*), a gear there opens Settings. Favourites and the other apps each keep their own order. The order is saved 0.7 s after your last move, and at once when edit mode ends or HearthNG leaves the screen. While *Order by last opened* is on the arrows do not move the app.

## Favourites: a short shelf

Hold **OK** on an app and choose **Add to favourites**. When you have favourites, the home screen shows **only that row**. Press **Down** to see the row with all the other apps. Press **Up** to hide it again. Remove a favourite the same way. Margins for this row are in **Settings, Look, Favourites**.

![Home row with a favourites row above it](../screenshots/home-favourites.jpg)

## Your own names and icons

In **Settings, Apps & Sources** hold **OK** on an app or an input, let go, then press **Down** to edit.

![Editing an app](../screenshots/edit-app-icon.jpg)

- **Name:** up to 24 letters. Inputs offer ideas (PlayStation 5, Xbox, Laptop ...). Press OK to type any name.
- **Icon:** pick one that comes with HearthNG, or **use your own picture** from a USB stick or the TV's storage (rooted TVs). A square picture is best. HearthNG shrinks it and keeps it inside its own settings, so you can pull the USB stick out afterwards.
- **Use the app's name and icon** puts the original back.

The names in the *Hide* and *Uninstall* menus stay the real names.

## Recent apps

Press **Back** on the home row to see the apps you used lately. **OK** opens one. **Hold OK** closes it. **Up** and OK on *Close all* closes them all. This needs a rooted TV. The panel size is in **Settings, Look, Recent apps**.

![Back opens the Recent apps panel](../screenshots/recents.jpg)

## When you press Home

**Settings, Look, Home row, When Home is pressed** decides where the highlight starts: on the **first app**, or on the app you **opened last** (that is the starting choice). Right after you switch the TV on, the highlight always starts on the first app until you open something. Press Home twice and it jumps to the first app.

## Quiet mode and OLED care

- **Quiet mode** hides the apps after a pause, so only the clock stays. Any key brings them back. Choose the pause (Off, or 10 to 60 seconds) in **Settings, Look, Quiet mode**. At first it is 20 seconds.
- **Dim the screen after** makes the whole picture darker some seconds later. **Dim strength** says how much (10 to 80 percent). This is a dark layer, the TV's backlight is not touched.
- **OLED shift** moves the clock and the apps a tiny bit every minute, so a still picture does not burn into an OLED screen. It is on at first.

![Quiet mode: only the clock stays](../screenshots/quiet-mode.jpg)

## Input status

Inputs with nothing plugged in are greyed out. On a rooted TV, the name of a connected device (for example your game console) shows under its input. Switch this in **Settings, Look, Home row, Show input status**.
